Home/FAQs

FAQs

How do I book an appointment? ▾
Click "Book" in the navigation or "Book a Service" on the home page. Follow the 3-step process to select your service, choose a date and time, then confirm.
What is your cancellation policy? ▾
You may cancel or reschedule up to 24 hours before your appointment at no charge. Cancellations within 24 hours may incur a fee.
Do you offer free shipping? ▾
Yes! Orders over ₱1,500 qualify for free standard shipping within Metro Manila. Provincial orders are charged a flat rate of ₱200.
How do I return a product? ▾
We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for unopened items in original packaging. Contact us at [email protected] to initiate a return.
